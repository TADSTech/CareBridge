# Executive Summary

**CareBridge turns healthcare access into healthcare understanding.**

It is an AI communication layer that helps patients and healthcare workers overcome language and health-literacy barriers during medical conversations.

The patient speaks in the language they are comfortable with. CareBridge turns their words into a clear clinical summary. The healthcare worker responds, and CareBridge converts that response into plain language, the patient's preferred language, or accessible voice output.

# Problem We Are Solving

Africa does not only have a healthcare access problem.

It has an **understanding problem**.

A patient in Lagos may speak Yoruba or Pidgin while their healthcare experience is conducted primarily in English. A patient may understand everyday English but struggle with words like “hypertension,” “contraindication,” or “dosage interval.”

For someone who is sick, elderly, has low literacy, or has an accessibility need, misunderstanding a healthcare conversation can have real consequences.

And that creates a contradiction:

**The system may provide the doctor.
The hospital may provide the medicine.
But the patient still may not understand the care.**

# How We Solve It

CareBridge creates a real-time communication bridge between both sides.

A patient speaks naturally.

**CareBridge listens → translates → summarizes → clarifies.**

The healthcare worker responds.

**CareBridge simplifies → translates → speaks or displays the response.**

This allows us to tackle language, literacy, and accessibility within the same product rather than treating them as separate problems.

CareBridge is delivered as an installable Progressive Web App (PWA): one URL, home-screen install on Android and iOS, offline-capable shell, and no app-store dependency. Combined with in-product accessibility controls (text size, high contrast, voice playback, reduced motion, keyboard navigation), it works on the low-end devices and uneven networks common across our target markets.

We start with high-impact African languages and expand across Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Uganda and beyond.

The business sustains itself through subscriptions from clinics and hospitals, enterprise deployments for healthcare organizations and NGOs, and API access for health-tech platforms.

Our goal is not to build another healthcare app.

**Our goal is to make the healthcare that already exists more accessible to the people who need it.**
